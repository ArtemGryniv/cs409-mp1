Best Study Spots on Campus

  • North
  • South
  • Quiet
  • Busy

North

Grainger Library

Great for locking in right before a CBTF test. Enjoy a collaborative space on the first and second floors or move up to a strictly quieter space on the higher floors.

CIF

Ideal for small groups working on an assignment. If you go after 5, it's likely you'll find an empty classroom with large tables and plenty of whiteboards.

Siebel Center for Computer Science

The basement is great for working with 1-2 people with comfy chairs and spacious tables. Plenty of whiteboards. Also enjoy cool study spots outside in the courtyard and on the balconies.

South

Funk Library

Enjoy a building with unique architecture and lots of quiet space. Has an older feel compared to some of the northern buildings.

Gies College of Business

Even if you're not a business major, check out this spot for its huge, tall open space with tables overlooking the back lawn and large center area from the second and third floors.

Siebel Center for Design

Great spot if you randomly just want to take a break from studying and see the multitude of things people are doing in the building. Lots of variety of spots (tables, couches, high tables, booths). Great if you enjoy random classical music being played.

Quiet

Main Library

Absolute silence. Find a large wooden table, sit down, turn on the lamp, and get to work.

Basement of Digital Computer Lab

A little sketchy looking, but it will motivate you to work hard and there are very few distractions that occur.

Newmark Civil Engineering Lab

Underrated spot with a few "easter egg" study spaces you can find that are very quiet and free of distractions.

Busy

Illini Union

Specifically the area by the Starbucks. There are constantly students coming in and out, having conversations at tables, and random events occurring. If you enjoy spontaneously stopping your work and watching a random dance performance, this is the spot for you.

ARC/CRCE

Both are gyms, what can you expect? They have tables by the entrances where you can people-watch while you study.

Lobbies of Dorms in Six-Pack

If you live here or know someone that does, you should check out the lobbies. There are quite the entertaining sights and activities that can be seen here as you crack down on your math problems.
